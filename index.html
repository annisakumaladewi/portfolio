Annisa Kumaladewi

Exploring the stories data can tell.

I'm someone who’s fascinated by how data can reveal patterns, challenge assumptions, and drive more informed decisions - especially in areas where it can improve lives or systems.

These days, I'm working as a Business Analyst at the International Planned Parenthood Federation. Day to day, that means using data visualisation and data science to support the organisation's mission around Sexual and Reproductive Health Rights. My love for data started during my time at University College London, where I studied Philosophy, Politics, and Economics with Social Data Science.

I'm comfortable working across Python, R, Tableau, PowerBI, SQL, QGIS, and STATA, and I'm currently learning d3.js(this page is proof of that!). I'm always curious, always learning, and open to conversations about data, social good, or anything in between.
